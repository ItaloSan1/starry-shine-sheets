# 07 — Maintenance

Service Manual 1268557GT Rev A4 defers detailed maintenance to Genie's separate
**Maintenance Manual for booms** (not among the three source docs). Items below
are from the Operator's Manual `[V]` and indexed Genie maintenance text `[S]`.

## Operator-level items (Operator's Manual pp.61–63)
- Engine oil level (engine off): 15W-40 shipped; cold: 10W-30 (Perkins 1104C), 5W-40 (Perkins 804D), 5W-30 (Deutz), 10W-30 (Cummins).
- Hydraulic oil level at sight gauge, boom stowed.
- Batteries: tight clean cables, hold-down, electrolyte to bottom of fill tube with distilled water; PPE.
- Engine coolant in recovery tank at NORMAL (liquid-cooled models).
- Scheduled quarterly, annual and two-year maintenance by trained, qualified technicians. Machines out of service more than three months need the quarterly inspection before return to service.

## Daily pre-operation inspection (Operator's Manual p.30) `[V]`
Manuals in the platform container; decals legible; engine oil, hydraulic oil, coolant, battery levels and leaks; inspect electrical components/wiring/cables, hydraulic hoses/fittings/cylinders/manifolds, fuel and hydraulic tanks, drive and turntable motors and hubs, boom wear pads, tires and wheels, engine, **limit switches and horn, rotation sensors, steer and axle sensors**, alarms/beacons, fasteners, platform mid-rail/gate; cracks in welds, dents, rust; covers latched.

## Daily function tests (Operator's Manual pp.31–38) `[V]`
Ground: LCD comes on with no error messages; E-stop kills engine and functions; axles-retracted interlock (booms will not raise/extend, turntable ±15°); extend axles from platform while driving; all boom functions cycle with enable button, none without; auxiliary power runs all boom functions; tilt sensor readouts X/Y and platform level degrees display; PRI BOOM ANGLE TO GRAVITY and SEC BOOM ANGLE display; secondary raises then extends, retracts then lowers.
Platform: E-stop; horn; alarm on any switch press; engine will not start with foot switch down; no function without foot switch; all functions cycle; all four steer modes turn the correct wheels; drive stops abruptly on centring; drive enable; limited drive speeds (1 ft/s raised/extended; 6 in/s jib and primary extended) else tag out; auxiliary power runs boom and steer; aircraft package.

## Interval items known from Genie maintenance text `[S]`
| Interval | Items |
|----------|-------|
| Daily / 8 h (Checklist A) | pre-operation inspection, function tests, engine maintenance checks, manuals and decals |
| Every 100 h | grease turntable rotation bearing and rotate gear |
| Quarterly / 250 h (A+B) | batteries, electrical wiring (apply dielectric grease to connectors `[F]`), exhaust, tires/wheels and **lug nut torque**, hydraulic oil visual, hydraulic filters, drive hub oil level |
| Semi-annual / 500 h (A+B+C) `[M]` | standard Genie schedule: engine service items, hydraulic filter replacement |
| Annual / 1000 h (A+B+C+D) | turntable rotation bearing bolts check, platform load sense calibration `[S]`, annual inspection (decal 52865) |
| Two years / 2000 h (A+B+C+D+E) `[M]` | hydraulic oil change / analysis, hub oil replacement |

Confirm `[M]` rows against the Genie Z-boom Maintenance Manual before quoting.
