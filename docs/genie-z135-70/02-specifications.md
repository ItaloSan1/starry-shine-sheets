Common to all: compression of lowest cylinder must be at least 75% of the highest; fan belt deflection 3/8 to 1/2 in (Perkins 1104C: 3/8 in); switch installation torque 8–18 ft-lb.

Glow plugs: Deutz models glow for 30 s after button; Perkins and Cummins hold 3–5 s. Restart delay disables start switch 3 s after a failed start; do not crank more than 15 s, wait 60 s. Below 20°F warm engine 5 min before operating. Below 0°F cold-start kit / booster battery may be needed. **Cold-storage fault:** if stored below 32°F with the secondary boom raised and extended the engine may not start due to a system fault; clear it by using emergency controls to raise the secondary boom until it begins extending. `[V]` (Op. manual p.47)

## Hydraulic oil (Service Manual pp.2–4; Op. manual p.62)
- Recommended: **Chevron Rando HD Premium, ISO VG 32, VI 200** (operator's manual decal 82366 says "Chevron Rykon Premium MV equivalent").
- Requirements: VI > 140; cleanliness ISO 15/13 minimum; water 250 ppm max.
- Optional, mineral: Shell Tellus S2 V 32, S2 V 46, S4 VX 32, Shell Donax TG (Dexron III), Chevron 5606A. Biodegradable: Petro-Canada Environ MV 46. Fire resistant: UCON Hydrolube HP-5046. Optional fluids need Genie Product Support approval and extra equipment.
- Chevron 5606A (ISO 15, VI 300) is **required** below 0°F / -17°C unless an oil heater is used; continued use above 32°F may damage components. Do not operate below -20°F / -29°C with Rando HD Premium MV. Do not operate above 120°F / 49°C ambient. If oil consistently exceeds 200°F / 90°C an oil cooler may be needed.
- Do not top off with incompatible fluids.
