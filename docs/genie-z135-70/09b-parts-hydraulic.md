# 09b — Hydraulic parts reference (Parts Manual 106877GT)

Every entry below is `[V]` — transcribed from the extracted text of Genie
Parts Manual **106877GT** in `extracted/106877.txt` (PDF in
`source/106877.pdf`). Part numbers, descriptions, quantities and
serial-number notes are quoted **exactly as printed**, including the
manufacturer's trailing asterisks (`*`, `**`, `***`, `****`), whose meaning is
not defined anywhere in the extracted text.

> **Revision caveat.** The README describes 106877 as "Rev G6, Jan 2013". The
> PDF actually ingested is **Rev H.04, July 2024, "From SN100 to 2000"**
> (cover page, p.1). Page numbers below are the printed page numbers of that
> revision, which coincide with the PDF page index (358 pages). Part numbers
> can differ from the 2013 printing — H.04 carries many later supersessions
> (e.g. swing drive 1255768, swivel 1253702, function manifold 119412,
> aftermarket filter elements).

**Reading conventions used in the manual.** An item number with a trailing
dash (`4-`, `10-`) is a sub-component or service part of the item above it.
`A-`/`B-` items are complete assemblies. A blank quantity means none was
printed. "to SN n" = up to and including serial n; "from SN n" = serial n and
later. Schematic item letters (e.g. `BQ`, `D`, `DF`) are printed only in the
steer/axle manifold table and in the from-SN-440 function manifold tables;
the exploded-view diagram pages (checked by rendering pp.220, 228, 246) carry
item numbers only.

## 1. Pumps (714.1, p.269) and auxiliary power unit (304.1, p.57)

The manual does not label the two main pumps "function" and "drive". The
descriptions themselves identify them: item 1 is the **lift (function) pump**
(fixed-displacement piston pump with a pump-control kit); item 2 is the
**Sauer-Danfoss M46 46 cc piston pump with EDC** (electronic displacement
control), i.e. the closed-loop **drive pump**. No separate **charge pump** is
listed anywhere in 106877GT — on an M46 the charge pump is internal to the
drive pump and is not broken out as a part.
